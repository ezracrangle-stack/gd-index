Interviews

  • p. 5 ↗ Amelia Morris & Michael Hutchens Brandes Investment Partners

    Two Brandes analysts, one on European consumer names and one on banks, on how the investment committees work. Watches of Switzerland and Slovenia's NLB are the examples.

    Companies: Watches of Switzerland, NLB InternationalFinancialsConsumer
  • p. 18 ↗ Jillian McIntyre 221B Capital Partners

    Jillian started 221B in 2024 after years at hedge funds and banks. She covers learning to short, her 5Ms framework, the Carvana call and a long case for Overstock.

    Companies: Carvana, Overstock Short sellingTechnologyBuilding a firm
  • p. 35 ↗ Hendrik Bessembinder Arizona State University

    The professor behind the finding that a small share of stocks creates most of the market's wealth. He talks about the losers, what diversification really protects against, and his new work on founder CEOs.

    Academic researchPortfolio construction

    David co-founded Giverny Capital Asset Management in 2020. He talks about judging management and three holdings: Progressive, Arista, and Constellation Software after Mark Leonard.

Student pitches

  • p. 26 ↗ Long dLocal By Bennett Lee, Srivats Mohan, Aaron Samuels and Oliver Suffern 2025 Booth MBA Stock Pitch Competition, 1st place

    An emerging-markets payments company where volume growth is picking up again, take-rate cuts are leveling off and a new management team is tightening execution.
  • p. 28 ↗ Long Advance Auto Parts 7.375% Senior Unsecured Notes due 2033 By Chidi Keng, Gabriel W Martins and James Michalski 2025 Chicago Booth Credit Pitch Competition, 1st place

    A credit pitch: the 2033 notes yield about 7.4%, and the team expects spreads to tighten as the business stabilizes.
  • p. 30 ↗ Long A.O. Smith By Craig Larkin, Olivia Qi and Elliot Tompras 2025 Darden at Virginia Investing Challenge, 1st place

    A 150-year-old water heater maker whose ties to plumbers and wholesalers keep its costs below rivals'.
  • p. 32 ↗ Long MercadoLibre By Victor D. Aguiar, Carolina S. Alzamora, Alejandro L. Dodds, Leonardo M. Fernandes and Rogério Rios Fall 2025 Security Analysis class, one of the two best presentations

    Expensive on next year's numbers. The team's 7 to 10 year case rests on MercadoLibre's scale in Latin American commerce and fintech.
