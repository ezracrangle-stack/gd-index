Student pitches

  • p. 34 ↗ Long RB Global By Xuechung (Elsa) Fu, Xutong Liu and Yifan Wang 2024 Neuberger Sustainable Investing Challenge, 1st place

    The largest auction platform for used heavy equipment, now also half of the US salvage-car duopoly after buying IAA.

    Eight years spent turning a 5-segment conglomerate into a pure building-products company. The team thinks the market hasn't caught up.

    India's leading food delivery app plus Blinkit in quick commerce. The stock fell about 35% when Eternal decided to spend heavily on Blinkit.
