Student pitches

  • p. 36 ↗ Long DoorDash By Zekun (ZK) Feng, Aaron Samuels and Elliot Tompras 19th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, 1st place

    More durable growth in US restaurants, better economics in new verticals and international, and margins the team thinks will widen as DoorDash scales.
  • p. 38 ↗ Long Baker Hughes By Cameron Degan, Greer Lafontant and Karl Eshwer 19th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, 2nd place

    The market still sees a cyclical oilfield supplier. The team sees an energy-infrastructure business whose installed equipment feeds years of service revenue.
  • p. 40 ↗ Long Celsius Holdings By Jyas Totlani and Hide Okada 19th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, 3rd place

    The market prices Alani fad risk, Costco private label and the integration as permanent problems. The team thinks all three are temporary.
  • p. 41 ↗ Long MSA Safety By EJ Karobath, Craig Larkin and Bob McGrane 19th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    A century-old maker of gear for workers in hazardous jobs, pitched as a quality pick-and-shovel compounder.
  • p. 42 ↗ Long Amadeus IT Group By Kabir Bajaj, Eithel Mc Gowen and Fran Tumajan 19th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    Room to grow in airline IT, margins going back to normal, and an AI threat the team thinks the market has overpriced.
  • p. 43 ↗ Long Uber Technologies By Dennis Zhuo 4th Annual Kawaja Growth Challenge, finalist

    Dennis thinks the selloff on robotaxi fears overshoots, and that Uber ends up as a partner to autonomous fleets.
