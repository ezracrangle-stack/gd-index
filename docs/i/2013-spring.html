Student pitches

  • p. 6 ↗ Long Motors Liquidation Company GUC Trust By David Magid 2013 Moon Lee Prize, winner

    A long on the trust units left from GM's bankruptcy, built on a lien dispute David expected the court to reject.
  • p. 8 ↗ Long Precision Castparts By Arjun Bhattacherjee 2013 Moon Lee Prize, finalist

    The Street has underestimated how well Precision Castparts deploys free cash flow and finds synergies.
  • p. 36 ↗ Long Hertz By Richard Hunt, Stephen Lieu and Rahul Raymoulik 2013 Pershing Square Challenge, winner

    The Dollar Thrifty merger finished ten years of consolidation in car rental, which the team thought the market underestimated. About 52% upside in 12 months.
  • p. 38 ↗ Long Advance Auto Parts By Joe Fleury, John Gallagher and Seth Kirner 2013 Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    Trading at a big discount to intrinsic value, with a three-year target of about $165.
  • p. 40 ↗ Long Dollar Tree By Jeremy Colvin, Eric Lai and Akhil Subramanian 2013 Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    The dollar-store chain with 4,500-plus US stores, pitched with about 40% upside including a recap funded with debt.
  • p. 42 ↗ Long Stanley Black & Decker By Arjun Bhattacherjee, Rory Ellison and Colin Kennedy 2013 Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    An asymmetric risk/reward, with a $107 target.
  • p. 44 ↗ Long Yum! Brands By Omar Elangbawy, Ranjan Ramchandani and Andrew Woodruff 2013 Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    Steady US cash flows and a wide international footprint, with the stock held down by food-safety and avian-flu scares in China.
