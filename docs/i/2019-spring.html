Student pitches

  • p. 15 ↗ Long Dollarama By Mingming Wu, Laurent Liu and K.Y. Wong 2019 Pershing Square Challenge, 1st place

    A long with friendly activism. Dollarama has 71% of Canada's dollar-store market, and the team argues analysts underestimate how far it can still grow.
  • p. 17 ↗ Long Align Technology By Michael Wooten

    Pitched for its end-to-end digital workflow and for running the largest 3D manufacturing operation in the world.
  • p. 20 ↗ Long Carsales.com By Tyler Redd

    An Australian car marketplace that draws more unique visitors relative to its home population than any of its peers.
  • p. 22 ↗ Long Dean Foods 6.50% Senior Unsecured Notes due 2023 By Mitchel Aulds-Stier, Karthik Kasibhatia, Angela Qin and James Shen 2019 UCLA Credit Pitch Competition, 1st place

    A credit pitch: the bonds traded at $66, and the team expected cost cuts and steadier dairy demand to pull them up.
