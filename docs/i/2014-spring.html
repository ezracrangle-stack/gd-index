Student pitches

  • p. 22 ↗ Long Allegion By Brian Waterhouse, Matt Ford and Øystein Kværner 2014 Pershing Square Challenge, 1st place

    Growth should pick up as nonresidential construction recovers, and the European business can close its gap with peers. About 50% upside to the end of 2016.
  • p. 24 ↗ Short Cablevision By Joe Goldschmid, Allen Keel and Mahmud Riffat 2014 Pershing Square Challenge, 2nd place

    Verizon FiOS was a real and growing threat, and the team saw about 52% return on the short.
  • p. 26 ↗ Long Carnival By Juliana Bogoricin, Charles Buaron and Ben Isaac 2014 Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    One-time setbacks hid the earnings power, with a two-year target about 53% above the price.
  • p. 28 ↗ Long Clean Harbors By Matt Bracewell, Patrick Enriquez-Fischer and Pavel Kaganas 2014 Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    A 12 to 18 month target 50% to 70% above the price, built on five points.
  • p. 30 ↗ Long Naspers By Carter Roehl, George Taras and Luke Tashie 2014 Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    The market valued Naspers' stakes in Tencent and Mail.ru at more than the whole company, so the rest came free.
