Interviews

  • p. 1 ↗ Paul B. Kazarian Japonica Partners Starts on the cover, continues on p. 5

    Paul went from corporate white knight in the 1980s to Fortune 500 CEO, and then became one of the largest holders of Greek government bonds, with $4 billion, during the euro crisis. He explains why he rejects the standard risk/return framework.

  • p. 1 ↗ Dana Bezerra, Preeti Bhattacharji & Nisha Prasad Heron Foundation Starts on the cover, continues on p. 25

    Heron aims to invest 100% of its capital in line with its mission. Its president and two CBS alumni explain how they analyze a company's social impact and where impact investing is headed.

Student pitches

  • p. 17 ↗ Short Peloton Interactive By Bruce Kim 2020 Artisan International Value Stock Pitch Challenge, finalist

  • p. 19 ↗ Long Rolls-Royce By Alvaro Pasquin Llorente 2020 Artisan International Value Stock Pitch Challenge, winner

  • p. 21 ↗ Long Nuance Communications By James Shen 2020 Artisan International Value Stock Pitch Challenge, finalist

  • p. 23 ↗ Long Etsy By Mingming Wu, Kyle Campbell and K.Y. Wong 2019 Neuberger Berman ESG Challenge, champion
