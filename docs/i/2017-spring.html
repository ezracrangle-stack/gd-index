Student pitches

  • p. 28 ↗ Long Yum China By Windsor Cristobal, Anji Lin and Isabella Lin 10th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, winner

    A two-year target 35% above the price, with earnings per share the team expected to grow 64% in three years.
  • p. 30 ↗ Long Alaska Air Group By Chris Waller, SK Lee and HK Kim 10th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    Alaska, Virgin America and Horizon together make the fifth-largest US carrier. The team saw 154% upside over 5 years.
  • p. 32 ↗ Long Corning By Griffin Dann, Joseph O'Hara and Vikas Patel 10th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    The team argues the market undervalues Corning's core assets and overlooks growth in optical fiber and Gorilla Glass.
  • p. 34 ↗ Long Dollarama By Gustavo Campanhã, Gilberto Giuzio and Thiago Maffra 10th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    New stores could triple earnings per share in five years, with five times as much reward as risk.
