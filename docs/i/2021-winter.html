Interviews

  • p. 4 ↗ Arthur Young Tensile Capital Management

    Arthur was a litigator in Miami before he became an investor. He explains how that helps him study problems after they show up, and walks through Avalara, Valvoline's quick-lube business and Dick's Sporting Goods.

    Companies: Avalara, Valvoline, Dick's Sporting Goods Special situationsTechnologyBuilding a firm
  • p. 29 ↗ John Huber Saber Capital Management

    John runs Saber on the original Buffett Partnership fee structure: no management fee. He goes deep on Etsy, explains how he thinks about Apple, and why he owns NVR.

    Companies: Etsy, Apple, NVR Quality compoundersBuilding a firm
  • p. 44 ↗ Wilmot Kidd & John Hill Central Securities

    Central Securities has been a closed-end fund since 1929. Wil recalls being the numbers guy at Hayden Stone when it took part in Intel's first financing, and John explains why the private insurer Plymouth Rock is about 20% of the fund.

    Companies: Plymouth Rock Quality compoundersJudging managementCareer advice
