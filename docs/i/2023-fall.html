Student pitches

  • p. 34 ↗ Long Lithia Motors By Thomas Schlabach, Nick Stern and Sam Hook 2023 Pershing Square Challenge, 1st place

    A single Oregon dealership from 1946 that became the largest auto dealer group in the US. The team saw the stock doubling in 4 years.
  • p. 36 ↗ Long PayPal By Jennifer Ma, Takuro Fujii and Mario Stefanidis 12th Annual Darden @ Virginia Investing Challenge, 1st place

    PayPal with a 2028 target of $110, about double the price at the time. Braintree's scale is a big part of the case.
  • p. 38 ↗ Long Vertiv By April Jiachen Li, Julie Zhou and Bhakti Thacker 2023 CSIMA Stock Pitch Challenge, 1st place

    Power and cooling gear for data centers. The team expects Vertiv to grow at 1.5 times its market as the sector consolidates.
