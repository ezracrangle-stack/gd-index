Student pitches

  • p. 16 ↗ Short Stericycle By Jade Hu, Asher Jacobs and Rana Pritanjali 11th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, winner

    New competition, a high fixed-cost base and a stretched balance sheet, with a target about 40% below the price.
  • p. 18 ↗ Short Credit Acceptance By Steve Cao, Winter Li and Tyler Redd 11th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, 2nd place

    Shorted with a target 33% below the price and a bear case of 71% downside.
  • p. 20 ↗ Short Spotify By Mike Allison, Eric Herzfeld and Michael Wooten 11th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, 3rd place

    Built on interviews with 23 music-industry experts, the team saw about 50% downside by the end of 2019.
  • p. 22 ↗ Short C.H. Robinson By Ryan Darrohn, Brad Headley and John White 11th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    A truckload broker whose spread the team expected to shrink as the market changes.
  • p. 24 ↗ Short Harvey Norman By Arthur Brousseau, Greg Doger de Speville and Neethling McGrath 11th Annual Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    An Australian retailer at the top of the cycle, facing Amazon Australia, with franchisee revenue and cash receipts drifting apart.
  • p. 26 ↗ Long Digicel 2020 notes By Jingjing Huang, Matthew Mann and Viraj Vora 2018 UCLA Credit Pitch Competition, winner

    A credit pitch on Digicel's bonds due 2020.
