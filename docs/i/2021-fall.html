Interviews

  • p. 4 ↗ Gavin Baker Atreides Management

  • p. 20 ↗ Rudi van Niekerk Desert Lion Capital

    Rudi came from private equity and launched Desert Lion in 2019 to invest in South Africa. He covers capital allocation there, Naspers and Prosus, and the country's place in platinum-group metals.

    Companies: Naspers InternationalPortfolio construction
