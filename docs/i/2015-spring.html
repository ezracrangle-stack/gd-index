Student pitches

  • p. 51 ↗ Long Altice By Brendan Dawson, Patrick McDonald and Moiz Valji 2015 Pershing Square Challenge, 1st place

    A telecom holding company with nearly 80% upside in the base case, and more from consolidation, mobile repair and further deals.
  • p. 53 ↗ Long Fiat Chrysler By Brandon Cohen, Christopher Crawford and Tyler Boone 2015 Pershing Square Challenge, 2nd place

    Fiat Chrysler earns nothing from Fiat or Chrysler. Ferrari, Jeep, Ram and Maserati alone are worth more than the market cap.
  • p. 55 ↗ Long HCA Holdings By Damian Creber and Michael Herman 2015 Pershing Square Challenge, 3rd place

    Being first or second in each local market gives HCA bargaining power with insurers.
  • p. 57 ↗ Long Genuine Parts By Lauren Harmon, Derek Johnson and Adam Kommel 2015 Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    The team wanted the auto parts business to stand alone, through a Reverse Morris Trust deal for the office unit and a tax-free spin of industrials.
  • p. 59 ↗ Long Precision Castparts By Aaron Purcell, Ben Hansen and Yinan Zhao 2015 Pershing Square Challenge, finalist

    The market leader in a business with real barriers to entry and a long record of strong free cash flow and capital allocation.
